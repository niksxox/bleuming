📖 Nihi's Pocket Notes — Care & Understanding

The Changing Garden (PCOS / Hormonal Rhythms)

• Varied Signals: PCOS can involve irregular periods, fatigue, skin changes, or mood shifts. Symptoms fluctuate and are not anyone's fault.

• No Single Formula: What works for one person's comfort may differ for another. Food and rest choices are supportive, never cures.

• Healthcare Partnership: Clear, neutral notes about your cycle and symptoms help doctors investigate without rushed guesswork.

The Tired Moon (Postpartum Emotional Health)

• Postpartum Reality: Hormonal drops, sleep disruption, and enormous shifts can bring baby blues or postpartum depression/anxiety.

• A Medical Condition, Not Failure: Postpartum mood conditions are common and treatable. They are never a measure of your love or capability.

• Caregivers Deserve Care: Asking for meals, rest, or professional guidance protects both parent and family.

The Foggy Forest (Emotional Grounding)

• 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding: Reconnecting with sensory details (sight, touch, sound) pulls focus away from spiraling anxiety loops.

• Permission to Pause: Pausing does not mean quitting. Gentle pacing honors your nervous system.

About Bleuming — Women's Wellbeing

Design Philosophy & Compassion

Bleuming is crafted as an emotionally intelligent, cozy exploration game. Rather than serving clinical quizzes or moralized health trackers, it teaches the art of distinguishing:

  • Sensations & Signals: What is my body communicating?
  • Associated Emotions: Am I feeling isolated, frustrated, or hurried?
  • Supportive Steps: Rest, asking a friend, or talking to a doctor.

Designed for women, young adults, and anyone navigating hormonal shifts, postpartum recovery, or daily overwhelm. Built with love & Y2K nostalgia.
